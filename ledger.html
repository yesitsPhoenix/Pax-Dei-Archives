How to Find Your Avatar ID

  1. 1 Open File Explorer and navigate to:
    %LocalAppData%\PaxDei\Saved\Logs\
  2. 2 Open PaxDei.log with a text editor.
  3. 3 Search (Ctrl+F) for Set Local Avatar Id
  4. 4 Copy the UUID that appears after it.

Quick Guide

Most transactions are handled via the sidebar on the left. It may be necessary to update gold manually (due to Dungeons, POIs, and gold pickup/chest deposits). In cases like this, use the sidebar modal to add a PVE transaction.

Adding Market Stalls

You can have multiple market stalls. Use the Market Stalls tab in the sidebar to add each stall with its location (valley, province, region). Once added, stalls appear as tabs when managing your listings. Select the correct stall before adding a listing.

Adding Listings

Click Add Listing to record a new market listing. Enter the item name, number of stacks, count per stack, and price per stack. Select the Market Stall the listing belongs to. Live market data and your sales history will appear to help guide your pricing.

Recording Purchases

Use the Add Purchase option in the sidebar to log items you have bought from other players' stalls. This deducts from your gold balance and keeps your transaction history accurate.

PVE Transactions

Use the Add PVE Transaction sidebar modal to manually record gold gained from Dungeons, POIs, chest deposits, or any other in-world gold pickup that isn't captured automatically.
